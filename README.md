Privacy Policy for LinkRide
Last updated: October 16, 2026

LinkRide ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LinkRide mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you:

Location Data: We may request access to your device's precise or approximate location to facilitate origin and destination selection when linking you to third-party ride services.

Usage & Device Information: Information on how the App is accessed and used, including device model, operating system version, and performance logs.

Payment Information: Payment transactions for service fees are securely processed by our third-party payment partner, Paystack. We do not store or collect your payment card details on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Deep Links
LinkRide acts as an intermediary app that connects users to third-party ride-hailing platforms (including Bolt, Yango, Uber, and Shaxi).

When you select a ride option, LinkRide deep-links or redirects you to the respective service's official app or web interface.

We do not store driver information or manage ride operations directly.

Your interactions with third-party ride platforms are governed by their respective privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Data
To provide and maintain our App features.

To process user service fees securely via Paystack.

To optimize app navigation and deep-link features.

To communicate app updates and support responses.

4. Third-Party Service Providers
We may employ third-party companies and individual service providers due to the following reasons:

Paystack: To securely process transactions and service fees.

Analytics Services: To monitor and analyze the use of our App.

These third parties have access to your Personal Information only to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

5. Data Security & Storage
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We implement commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your personal data.

6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
